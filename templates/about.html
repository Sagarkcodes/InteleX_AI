About InteleX AI

A next-generation AI Interview Assessment System designed to elevate confidence, improve communication, and deliver real-time insights — built for students, job seekers, and professionals.

What We Do

InteleX AI simulates a real interview experience using advanced machine learning and AI models. It evaluates your tone, clarity, communication skills, confidence, and responsiveness — all while generating tailored questions based on your resume and specific skills.

Key Features

🎤

Voice Personality Analysis

Analyze tone, pitch, pacing, clarity, and map them to Big Five Personality Traits.

📄

Resume-Based Questioning

AI reads your resume and generates role-based, skill-appropriate interview questions.

🎥

Video Interview Simulation

Face-to-face style interview simulation with camera, timing, and real-time prompts.

⚡

Real-Time AI Responses

AI-driven interview flow with instant question follow-ups and dynamic interactions.
