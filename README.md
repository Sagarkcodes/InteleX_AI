\# 🧠 InteleX\_AI

\### AI-Based Interview Assessment System



InteleX\_AI is an intelligent interview evaluation platform that simulates a real HR interview using Artificial Intelligence.  

It analyzes \*\*voice patterns\*\*, \*\*speech tone\*\*, and soon even \*\*facial expressions\*\* to assess a candidate’s personality traits and communication style.



---



\## 🚀 Features

\- 🎤 \*\*Voice-Based Personality Analysis\*\* – detects Big Five personality traits from voice tone.

\- 🗣️ \*\*AI HR Agent\*\* – interactive voice assistant that asks interview questions.

\- 📄 \*\*Resume Upload\*\* – candidate uploads their resume before the test.

\- 💬 \*\*Text + Voice Interaction\*\* – HR speaks and displays questions simultaneously.

\- 🧍‍♂️ \*\*Video Interview (Coming Soon)\*\* – emotion and facial expression detection using computer vision.
